## Task: after-the-close note

The China/HK markets just closed and one or more watchlist stocks triggered a price signal (listed under "Today's price signals" — limit crossings, big moves, new 52-week lows, volume spikes). Each signal comes with an attribution computed from the data: market-wide, sector-wide (the rest of the watchlist moved similarly), or stock-specific.

For each signalled stock, in order of importance to this investor:
1. One bold line: name, move, and the one-phrase reason (e.g. "**CATL (03750) −7.3% — AI-hardware selloff spilled into batteries; no company news**").
2. Two to four bullets: the likely cause (check filings and headlines in the bundle first, then search the web if the cause isn't clear — especially for stock-specific moves); whether it touches the thesis; what it means for an entry given their limit and rules (a market-wide dip on intact fundamentals is the setup they're waiting for; a spike is not to be chased; a stock-specific drop needs the reason understood first).

Group stocks that moved for the same reason into one item rather than repeating yourself. If the whole watchlist fell with the market, lead with one line on the market driver.

End with a single line starting "**Bottom line:**" — what, if anything, deserves their attention or action this week.

Keep the whole message under ~250 words unless several genuinely separate things happened.
