## Task: material filing alert

One or more watchlist companies just published exchange filings that a rule-based filter marked as material (listed under "Exchange filings"; the opening text of each filing is included where available). Titles are in Chinese; translate them.

For each filing (group several filings from the same company into one item):
1. One bold line: company (ticker), what the filing is in plain English, and the key figure if there is one. E.g. "**Northern Rare Earth (600111) — Q1–Q3 profit pre-announced +135–139% y/y**".
2. Two to four bullets:
   • What it actually says — figures, counterparties, sizes, dates — from the filing text. If the text isn't included, use the headlines or fetch the filing link; say if you couldn't confirm details.
   • Why it matters: is it new (vs already-known guidance or earlier filings in the bundle), how big relative to the company, and what it signals (e.g. a buyback near 52-week lows, a placement that dilutes holders, insiders selling).
   • Fit with the thesis and with the price action in the watchlist table (e.g. "results up strongly while the stock is −9% this month — fundamentals and price have diverged").
   • What to do with it, if anything, given their limit and rules.

Keep it under ~200 words per company. Skip anything that turns out on reading to be routine, and say so in one line.
